How to Play

1. Choose your experience: Guided Journey or Free Exploration.

2. Take turns answering each question. Listen with curiosity, not judgment.

3. You can always pass, skip a challenge, or stop playing. No explanation needed.

4. Guided Journey takes you through four levels, with an optional fifth chapter.

5. In Free Exploration, choose your level, change direction, or let Surprise Me decide.

6. After Dark is optional and requires both players' agreement before entering.

7. Sharing a fantasy never means agreeing to act on it.

8. No right answers. No pressure. Just us.
